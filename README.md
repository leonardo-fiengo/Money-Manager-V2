# Money Manager

Run the local app with `.venv\Scripts\python.exe run.py`, then open http://127.0.0.1:5000. The launcher processes due payments at startup and every minute. Page, API, health and asset requests do not process scheduled payments. A separate scheduler can call `flask --app app process-scheduled` when using a different server.

Build the Windows app with `powershell -File build_executable.ps1`. The executable is `dist\MoneyManager.exe`; its data lives beside it in `dist\data`. Source runs use the root `data` directory. Close an older running version before launching an update.

Money is stored in integer cents. On the first updated launch, an atomic migration preserves record IDs and relationships and converts legacy amounts using decimal rounding. Generated major-unit columns retain compatibility with existing read queries. A local SQLite snapshot is saved before the migration, once each day the app runs, and before every restore.

Settings → Data & tools → Backup & restore exports all records and merchant logos. Both JSON exports and downloaded ZIP snapshots can be uploaded, validated and reviewed before replacing records. Restore first saves the current database and logos. Local snapshots are in `data\backups`; keep an exported copy on another device for protection against disk loss. The installation's session key in `data\.secret-key` is private and is intentionally excluded from exports.

Foreign-currency transactions retain the conversion rate, source and rate date used when saved. If live rates are unavailable, the app labels the conversion as estimated. Editing a description or category keeps that original conversion. Charts are bundled locally and work without a CDN connection.

The Overview is a dark financial dashboard with a ledger-derived balance chart, month-to-date KPIs, scrollable account tiles, upcoming payments, cash flow, budgets, review insights, and recent transactions. The balance chart includes all accounts, signed credit card liabilities, saved EUR conversions, and reconciliation adjustments on their statement dates. Internal transfers cancel out. Opening balances form the baseline because the current schema does not store their effective dates. Future-dated posted entries require review before a historical chart is shown.

Safe to Spend reuses the existing 30-day planning calculation: active liquid balances less savings reservations, card debt, and planned outflows, without counting ordinary card settlements twice. Investment balances and expected income are excluded. An unpaid tax or borrowed loan due within 30 days or overdue shows a review state, since those trackers cannot reliably be matched to scheduled payments. Your editable widget layout remains available at `/?view=workspace`; saving the visual editor opens that workspace.

Styles are split into `static/css/tokens.css` (one palette per theme), `money-manager.css` (shared controls and feature screens), `accounts.css` (account and savings views), `workspaces.css` (navigation and workspace layouts), and `today.css` (financial overview, custom workspace, and visual editor). Interface assets use modification timestamps for cache invalidation. The overview uses the bundled Chart.js library and read-only `/overview/balance` and `/overview/cash-flow` endpoints.

Run regression checks with `.venv\Scripts\python.exe -m unittest discover -s tests`. Tests use temporary databases. Browser verification should also use a copied database rather than creating review transactions in personal records.

Settings & tools now links to transaction rules, merchant aliases, import history and bank profiles, transfer matching, reconciliation history, transaction recovery, and daily net worth history. Sidebar destinations have no back arrow; secondary screens return to the page you opened them from, with a safe parent-page fallback.

CSV importing accepts UTF-8, Windows-1252, and BOM-marked UTF-16; detects delimiters and decimal conventions; and suggests English or Italian date/description columns. Map and review ambiguous dates before confirming. Save a named profile to automatically reuse the mapping for the same account and column headings. Rules have priorities, combined conditions, bounded regular expressions, actions, and a preview of up to 5,000 existing records. The first enabled matching rule suggests changes in the import preview. Correcting a merchant learns its original statement description as an alias.

Import fingerprints use the original bank values, so rule renaming does not defeat duplicate detection. Duplicate rows remain unchecked unless explicitly overridden. Completed batches with unchanged records can be rolled back; changed tags, splits, receipts, settlements, linked payments, or reconciliation locks require review first. Removed records are retained under Transaction recovery, including receipts and pending card settlements.

Match imported outgoing/incoming pairs only after reviewing that they are a movement between your own accounts. Matching keeps balances intact and excludes the pair from income and expenses; unmatching restores the original records. Refunds, reimbursements, and chargebacks link to an original expense and reduce reported spending. Credit card settlements retain explicit links to purchases.

Balance checks accept a statement through date. Matched or adjusted sessions close and lock financial fields of their transactions; multiple overlapping sessions retain their own membership. Reopen every relevant session before changing a checked record or an account opening balance. Reopening retains recorded adjustments. Difference previews identify similarly sized entries and possible duplicates with correction links.

The forecast simulates 30/60/90/180/365 days using posted balances, pending payments, recurring income and expenses, future card settlements, and a 90-day discretionary spending average. Saved scenarios model monthly income, spending, and investment changes without writing transactions. Per-account projections show scheduled activity; the total chart also includes average spending and scenario changes. Foreign recurring payments retain their original currency and a saved EUR estimate; generated payments save the rate used when created.

Subscriptions suggests recent payments with regular weekly/monthly/yearly intervals and similar amounts, shows annual and monthly cost, price changes, and the next expected date, and lets you track a reviewed pattern as a recurring rule. Recurring rules have occurrence history; removed generated payments are not generated again. Savings goals support target dates and estimated completion from recent contributions. Transaction details support notes and PDF/image/text receipts up to 5 MB. Accounts can be archived and reactivated while retaining their history. The workspace editor in Settings also chooses dashboard widgets; Ctrl+K and B remain available for navigation and privacy.

Snapshots carry a schema version and include receipts and merchant logos. SQLite integrity and foreign-key checks run after migrations; a separate snapshot is saved before upgrading an existing database. SQLite uses WAL, a writer timeout, and atomic financial operations. Transaction listing uses FTS5 search and server pagination.

Click an account from Accounts or Overview to see its balance, recent activity (including incoming transfers), monthly spending, category breakdown, and balance history. Account checks can open directly for that account. Missing card numbers and expiry dates are not fabricated.

Savings pots reserve existing money across one or more active bank, cash, prepaid card, or wallet accounts. Enter each source amount under "Where from?"; the total must match the contribution. Reservations leave balances unchanged. Releasing money reduces the selected source reservations. Existing pots without sources must have their reserved money attributed before further contributions or spending. Completed pots offer "Spend them already": tick the checkbox and record spending to create one EUR expense per source account, release the reservations, and mark the pot spent. Spending is atomic and cannot run twice. It records purchases in the app; it does not send payments. Delete releases an active pot's reservations without changing balances, preserves any recorded expenses, and allows restoration when enough money remains available. Backup schema 12 includes source allocations, movement sources, spent/deleted state, and spending references; older backups still restore with unattributed pots.

Backup & restore can enable encrypted automatic snapshots with a passphrase of at least 12 characters. New files use the .mmbackup extension; existing ZIPs and JSON exports remain plain. Encryption uses authenticated Fernet with an Argon2id-derived key; see https://cryptography.io/en/latest/fernet/. Only a locally wrapped derived key is retained in data/.backup-encryption.json, tied to data/.secret-key. Keep the passphrase to restore on another installation or after changing it. Encrypted files can be uploaded with the passphrase and reviewed before restore. Session and encryption configuration files are excluded from exports and source control.

The main navigation is Today, Activity, Plan, and Insights, with financial Accounts directly accessible. The global navbar has an icon for Settings, Add transaction, a personal Account profile, and a theme toggle. Mobile uses a bottom navigation bar. The personal profile stores a display name locally with the app data and includes shortcuts to appearance, privacy, payment preferences, and backups; it does not create an online login.

Ctrl+K opens a rectangular page launcher with icon tiles for the main pages, quick actions, and account tools. Search filters names and related keywords, arrow keys move between tiles, Enter opens a destination, and Esc closes the launcher. If no page matches, it offers a transaction search. The grid adapts to smaller screens and both themes.

Today highlights available cash after goal reservations, existing credit card debt, and known outgoing payments over 30/60/90 days. Investments and future income are excluded; card repayments are not deducted twice. Any planned payment beyond existing and newly planned card debt is reserved as additional outgoing cash. A computed status and at most three prioritized review actions explain what needs attention. A shared timeline combines planned income, payments, settlements, budget checkpoints, and goal milestones. This metric is based on recorded commitments, so unrecorded bills and everyday spending can change it.

Today → Edit your workspace (also under Settings → Workspace) opens a visual widget editor. Add or remove any of seven widgets, move them up or down, and choose half or full width while previewing current data. Save applies the layout; Reset to default prepares the original arrangement for saving. Unsaved changes trigger a browser reminder before leaving. Widget order and sizes are included in backups, and older four-panel preferences retain the core summary cards. Amount masking preserves nested formatting when hiding and revealing balances.

Plan combines a monthly income plan, budget health, goals, the shared timeline, and forecast warnings. Its Overview, Budget, Calendar, Recurring, Goals, and Forecast tabs preserve the existing detailed tools. Monthly remainder subtracts actual spending, planned purchases, unallocated budget capacity, and net active goal contributions, without double counting planned category bills or spent goals.

Activity has All, Needs review, Pending, Transfers, Imports, and Recovered/deleted views. Review suggests categories and merchants, supports Apply & next, J/K and Enter shortcuts, and safe bulk categorization of up to 50 uncategorized imported expenses. Review changes metadata without moving money. Its transaction side panel contains edit, notes, receipts, splits, tags, and related-payment controls. Import history, rollback, transfer matching, recovery, and rule creation are available in context. Settings groups preferences, organization, review, and data, with specialist tools in a disclosure.

Account rows are grouped as Everyday, Credit & settlements, Savings, Investments, and Archived, and show available money after reservations, upcoming activity, last balance checks, and warnings. Account detail includes its upcoming activity and reserved goals. Backup schema 13 adds account roles and the local personal profile; older backups remain supported. Migration adds these fields while preserving existing financial records.

Debts shows both I owe and Owed to me, including existing loans, while Loans has its own sidebar destination. Contacts live under your personal Account, with name, email, phone, and notes. Select a contact instead of a merchant when recording a payment; contact activity, archived contacts, and linked loan records retain their history. Your personal profile supports a photo, email, and phone. Money inbox is in the top navbar. Settings opens a hub with individual pages for appearance/privacy, workspace, payments, organization, review, and data/tools. On mobile, the navbar page launcher opens the same icon grid as Ctrl+K.

Taxes tracks amounts, deadlines, and recorded payments by tax year, with upcoming/overdue alerts in Money inbox. Its Italy IRPEF estimator uses verified 2024–2026 progressive brackets and manually entered deductible expenses, tax credits, local taxes, and withholding. Employment/pension relief, INPS, special regimes, and VAT are not calculated automatically. Save estimates to retain their inputs, results, and rule version in the yearly archive; adding a tracked obligation asks for a name and deadline. The current year advances automatically and past records stay available. Future calculator years require verified rates; the tracker can record them already. Debt repayments and tax payments update their trackers; record the matching transaction in Activity to change account balances. Contacts, profile photos, taxes, payments, and saved estimates are included in backups.
